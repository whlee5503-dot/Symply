# Symply Account and Data Deletion

Symply is operated by PublicHealth Tech Lab. This page explains how to delete your account and data, what is deleted, and what may be retained.

## 1. Delete in the app

1. Sign in to Symply.
2. Open **Settings** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete Account** at the very bottom.
4. Read the notice and tap **Delete permanently**.

You are signed out automatically when deletion is finished. Guest accounts can be deleted the same way.

## 2. Request deletion by email if you cannot use the app

If you cannot sign in to the app, email **contact@phtlab.org**.

- Subject: Account deletion request
- To verify it is you, send the email from **the address you used to sign up for Symply**.
- Once we confirm the request, we delete the account without undue delay and reply with the result.

Guest accounts cannot be verified by email, so please delete them inside the app.

## 3. Data that is deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently deleted from our servers and cannot be recovered.

- Account information (name, email address, user ID, sign-in details)
- Daily symptom, pain and fatigue scores, notes, trigger information and cycle records
- Profile settings such as selected chronic conditions, medications and visit dates

## 4. Data that may be retained

Only if you have a Symply Pro subscription history, the following transaction records are kept separately after account deletion as required by the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (Korea).

- Records on contracts or withdrawal of offers: 5 years
- Records on payment and provision of services: 5 years
- Records on consumer complaints or dispute handling: 3 years

We do not hold card or other payment details ourselves; they are processed by our payment provider, Polar. Users without a subscription history have no separately retained data.

## 5. Good to know

- Deleted data cannot be restored.
- **A Pro subscription is not cancelled automatically when you delete your account.** To cancel it, email contact@phtlab.org.
- Data sent for an AI pattern analysis is processed only temporarily to produce the analysis; see section 3 of the Privacy Policy.
- Usage statistics for improving the service are collected only in aggregate form and do not include your user ID.

Contact: contact@phtlab.org
