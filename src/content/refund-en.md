# Symply Refund and Cancellation

This page explains how to cancel a Symply Pro subscription and how refunds work. Symply is operated by PublicHealth Tech Lab.

## 1. About the subscription

- Symply Pro is a monthly subscription that renews automatically each month.
- The price is KRW 4,900 per month in Korea and USD $3.99 per month elsewhere. The amount shown at checkout is the amount charged.
- We do not currently offer an annual plan or a free trial.
- Payments are processed by our payment processor (Polar) in a web browser only, not inside the Google Play app. We do not store card or other payment details ourselves.

## 2. Refunds

- If you request a refund **within 7 days of the payment date**, we refund the payment in **full**.
- After 7 days from the payment date, payments are not refundable. Duplicate charges and charges caused by a service error are reviewed and handled individually.
- Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Depending on your card issuer or payment method, it may take some time to appear.

## 3. How to request a refund

Email **contact@phtlab.org**.

- Subject: Refund request
- Please write from the email address you used to sign up for Symply.
- If you have it, include the payment date and the receipt you received by email.

We will review your request and reply by email with the result.

## 4. Cancelling your subscription

- You can cancel at any time. After you cancel, you will not be charged **from the next payment** onward.
- You can keep using Pro until the period you have already paid for ends.
- To cancel, email **contact@phtlab.org** with the subject "Cancel subscription", from the email address you used to sign up for Symply.
- Cancelling does not by itself refund a payment you have already made. Refunds follow the rules in section 2.

## 5. Deleting your account and your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Pro subscription. Please ask us to cancel the subscription before you delete your account. See [Account and data deletion](/delete-account) for details.

Contact: contact@phtlab.org
